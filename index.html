Everything you need to know

The details, made simple.

We want everyone to have a fair, welcoming, and fun weekend. Please read the sample event requirements before signing up.

Registration & eligibility

  • Who can join: Current undergraduate or postgraduate students from any recognized institution.
  • Team size: 2–4 students. One person should register as the team lead.
  • Entry fee: Free for all selected participants. In-person attendance is required.
  • Apply by: 10 January 2027. Places are limited; registration is subject to confirmation.
  • Bring along: Student ID, laptop, charger, and any hardware your team needs.

Build guidelines

  • Build a new project during the event. Existing libraries and open-source tools are welcome; disclose what you use.
  • Submit a working demo, source-code link, short project description, and a clear team contribution summary.
  • Use only data and assets you have permission to use. Protect personal information and respect intellectual property.
  • Judging considers usefulness, originality, technical execution, responsible design, and the quality of your demo.
  • Be kind and inclusive. Harassment, unsafe projects, or unfair conduct can result in disqualification.

Sample event plan: organizers should confirm the final rules, accessibility and accommodation process, campus access, and judging details before publishing.
